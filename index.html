The flyer lists beach camping, boat ride, movie night, games, bonfire, quad biking, horse riding, refreshments and networking. Confirm which activities are included in the ticket and whether any have separate charges.

Absolutely—you can use the event to meet people and make new connections. If you have questions about group arrangements or safety, contact the organiser before booking.
